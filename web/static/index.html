      About TransitWise

      TransitWise plans step-free trips door to door: rolling, buses, subway, Commuter Rail and ferries, with live MBTA times. Every trip is checked against live elevator outages, moved or closed stops and service suspensions, and rerouted when something is broken.

      • Rolling routes come from OpenStreetMap and may miss broken curb cuts or construction.
      • Trips planned for later are checked against alerts active right now.
      • Always confirm with MBTA: mbta.com/alerts/access or 617-222-3200.
